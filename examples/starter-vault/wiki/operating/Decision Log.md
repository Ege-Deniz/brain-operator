# Decision Log

Open and closed decisions. Once a decision is closed, cite it instead of re-litigating.

## Format

```markdown
### <Decision Title>
- **status**: open | closed | revisit
- **deadline**: YYYY-MM-DD (only for open)
- **owner**: <name>
- **context**: <one paragraph — what's at stake>
- **options**:
  - A. <option>
  - B. <option>
- **decision**: <only when closed — which option and why>
- **flip criteria**: <conditions under which this should be reopened>
```
